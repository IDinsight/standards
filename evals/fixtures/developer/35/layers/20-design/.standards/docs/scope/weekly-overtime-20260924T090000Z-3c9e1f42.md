# Weekly Overtime

## Goal

Show how much each ISO week exceeds the standard 40-hour week so that
overtime can be reviewed from the same summary used for totals.

## Constraints

- The standard working week is 40 hours (2400 minutes).
- Weeks follow ISO 8601 (Monday start, week-numbering year).

## Non-goals

- Daily overtime rules, pay rates, or currency calculations.
- Changes to how entries are entered or validated.

## Work

### 1. Weekly overtime in the summary

**Intent:** Let a reader see each week's worked minutes and the part beyond
40 hours without computing it by hand.

**Done when:**

- `AC-001`: The summary reports, for each ISO week with entries, the worked minutes and the overtime minutes beyond 40 hours in that week.
- `AC-002`: A week with 40 hours or less reports zero overtime minutes.
- `AC-003`: The existing total and per-day figures are unchanged for the same entries.

**Depends on:** None
