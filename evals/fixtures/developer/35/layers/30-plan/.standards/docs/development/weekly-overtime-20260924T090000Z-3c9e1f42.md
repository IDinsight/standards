### DEV-002 — Weekly totals and overtime in summarize

`Status`: `PENDING` `Depends On`: `DEV-001`
`Acceptance`: `AC-001, AC-002, AC-003`

**Goal**

Extend `summarize` with a `weeks` property holding `minutes` and
`overtimeMinutes` per ISO week, using the 2400-minute threshold.

**Affected Area**

`src/summary.js`.

**Expected Outcome**

unchanged for the same entries.

**Self-Check**

Planned: from the project root, `node --test test/summary.test.js` (existing
suite must stay green) and a `node -e` sanity call with one week above and one
