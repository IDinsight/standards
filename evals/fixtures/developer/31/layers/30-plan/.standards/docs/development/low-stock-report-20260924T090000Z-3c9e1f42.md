### DEV-001 — Select and order low-stock items

`Status`: `PENDING` `Depends On`: `NONE`
`Acceptance`: `AC-001, AC-002`

**Goal**

Provide the selection the report prints: items at or below their reorder
level, in the order the design defines.

**Affected Area**

`inventory/stock.py`

**Expected Outcome**

`low_stock(items)` returns the items with `quantity <= reorder_level`, sorted
by quantity and then SKU, without changing its input.

**Self-Check**

Planned: `python3 -m pytest -q` from the project root to confirm existing
behavior is unchanged, plus a sanity call of `low_stock` on a few sample
items, including one exactly at its reorder level. Not yet run.

### DEV-002 — Add the low-stock subcommand

`Status`: `PENDING` `Depends On`: `DEV-001`
`Acceptance`: `AC-001, AC-002, AC-003`

**Goal**

Expose the selection as `inventory low-stock <csv>` with the design's output
format and empty-result message.

**Affected Area**

`inventory/cli.py`

**Expected Outcome**

The subcommand prints one tab-separated line per selected item, or
`No items at or below reorder level.` when there are none, and exits with
status 0.

**Self-Check**

Planned: `python3 -m pytest -q` from the project root, then
`python3 -m inventory.cli low-stock <sample csv>` against one file with
low-stock items and one without. Not yet run.
