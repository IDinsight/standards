# Low-Stock Report

## Goal

Let the stock clerk see which items need reordering without scanning the whole
CSV file.

## Constraints

- Reads the same CSV format the `summary` command reads.

## Non-goals

- Placing orders or changing the CSV file.

## Work

### 1. Low-stock command

**Intent:** Show the items that have reached their reorder level.

**Done when:**

- `AC-001`: `inventory low-stock <csv>` lists every item whose quantity is at
  or below its reorder level, one per line, with its SKU, name, quantity, and
  reorder level.
- `AC-002`: Listed items appear in ascending order of quantity, then SKU.
- `AC-003`: When no item is at or below its reorder level, the command prints
  `No items at or below reorder level.` and exits with status 0.

**Depends on:** None
