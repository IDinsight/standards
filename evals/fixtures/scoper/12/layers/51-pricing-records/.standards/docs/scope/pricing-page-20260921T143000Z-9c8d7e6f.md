# Pricing Page

## Goal

Publish a public pricing page so prospects can compare plans before they start
a trial.

## Non-goals

- Checkout or payment collection.

## Work

### 1. Plan comparison

**Intent:** Let prospects see what each plan costs and includes.

**Done when:**

- `AC-001`: The pricing page lists the Starter, Team, and Business plans with their monthly prices.
- `AC-002`: Each plan on the pricing page links to trial signup.

**Depends on:** None
