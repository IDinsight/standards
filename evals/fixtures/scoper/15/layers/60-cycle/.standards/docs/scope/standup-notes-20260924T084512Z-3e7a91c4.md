# Team Standup Notes

## Goal

Give one team a single place to post a short daily standup update and to read
everyone's updates for a day, so the team can stay informed without holding a
meeting every morning.

## Constraints

- One team uses the app; there is no separation between multiple teams.

## Non-goals

- Chat or email integrations and notifications.
- Reporting, analytics, or trends across days.
- Account management beyond identifying who posted an update.

## Work

### 1. Post a daily update

**Intent:** Let each team member share what they did, what they plan to do, and
what is blocking them, without waiting for a meeting.

**Done when:**

- `AC-001`: A team member can post an update for the current day with three
  parts: yesterday, today, and blockers.
- `AC-002`: An update whose three parts are all empty is rejected with a message
  and nothing is saved.

**Depends on:** None

### 2. Read the team's day

**Intent:** Let anyone on the team catch up on a day's updates in one place.

**Done when:**

- `AC-003`: Anyone on the team can view all updates for a given day, ordered by
  posting time, each showing who posted it.
- `AC-004`: Updates from earlier days remain viewable by choosing a date.

**Depends on:** 1

## Assumptions

- Team members identify themselves by name; stronger sign-in is not needed for
  this team.
