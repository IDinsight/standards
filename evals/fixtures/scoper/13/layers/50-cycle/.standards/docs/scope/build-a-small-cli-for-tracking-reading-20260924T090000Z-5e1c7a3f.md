# Reading List CLI

## Goal

Give one person a quick way, from a terminal, to keep track of books that were
recommended to them and which of those they have finished.

## Non-goals

- Sharing or syncing lists between people or machines.
- Ratings, reviews, or recommendations.

## Work

### 1. Keep a reading list

**Intent:** Capture recommended books before they are forgotten and see what
is still to read.

**Done when:**

- `AC-001`: A user can add a book with a title and an author to their reading
  list.
- `AC-002`: A user can list the books on their reading list, showing whether
  each one is unread or finished.

**Depends on:** None
