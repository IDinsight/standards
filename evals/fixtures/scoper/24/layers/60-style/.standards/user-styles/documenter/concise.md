# Concise

For guides written for people running the tool.

- Phrase every section heading as a question the reader would ask, for example "How do I export one month?".
- Keep sentences under 15 words. No introductions or recaps.
- Use numbered steps, one copy-paste command per step, followed by what the reader should see.
- Prefer the reader's words ("month", "file") over internal names.
- End the page with a single line starting "Stuck?" that names one place to look next.
