# Invoice Copy

## Goal

Billing staff re-create recurring or corrected invoices by retyping the
customer, terms, and line items of an earlier invoice. Let them copy an
existing invoice into a new draft that they can then issue as usual.

## Constraints

- Existing invoice numbering, lifecycle, and payment behavior stay unchanged.
- The copy is available through the existing HTTP API.

## Non-goals

- Editing line items on a draft.
- Automatic or scheduled recurring invoices.
- Copying payments or any payment history.

## Work

### 1. Copy an invoice into a new draft

**Intent:** Staff start a new invoice from an existing one instead of
retyping it.

**Done when:**

- `AC-001`: Staff can copy any existing invoice, whatever its status, into a
  new invoice through the API; the copy has the same customer, payment terms,
  and line items as the source.
- `AC-002`: The copy is a draft with a new invoice number from the normal
  sequence and an issue date of the day it was copied.
- `AC-003`: The copy has no payments and its balance equals its total; the
  source invoice, its status, and its payments are unchanged.
- `AC-004`: Copying an invoice number that does not exist returns not found
  and creates no invoice.

**Depends on:** None
