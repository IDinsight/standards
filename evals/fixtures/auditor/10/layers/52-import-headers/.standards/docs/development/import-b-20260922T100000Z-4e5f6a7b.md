`Status`: `DONE` `Depends On`: `NONE` `Acceptance`: `EXPEDITED_REQUEST`

**Goal**

Accept product CSV files whose `sku`, `name`, and `price` columns appear in
any order or letter case, as exported by the supplier spreadsheets.

**Affected Area**

`src/import.js` (`parseCsv`) and `test/import.test.js`.

**Expected Outcome**

<name>`; cell values are trimmed; whitespace-only lines are skipped.
