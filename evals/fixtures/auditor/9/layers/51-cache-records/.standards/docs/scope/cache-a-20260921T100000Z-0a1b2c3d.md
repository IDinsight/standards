# Catalog Cache Invalidation

## Goal

After a product is created or updated, catalog reads reflect the change
immediately instead of waiting for cached entries to expire.

## Non-goals

- Changing the cache TTL or moving the cache out of process.
- Changing the importer.

## Work

### 1. Fresh catalog reads after upsert

**Intent:** Stock counts shown from the catalog must not lag behind imports or
manual updates.

**Done when:**

- `AC-001`: After `Catalog.upsert`, `Catalog.list()` includes the new or
  updated product without waiting for the cache TTL.
- `AC-002`: After `Catalog.upsert`, `Catalog.get(sku)` returns the updated
  product.

**Depends on:** None
