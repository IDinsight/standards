# Intermittent 504s on POST /quotes

Opened: 2026-09-22 (support escalation)
Status: open

## Symptoms

- Checkout receives `504 Gateway Timeout` from `POST /quotes` on
  `quotes.parcelwise-prod.net`. Response body: `{"error":"rates_unavailable"}`.
- Intermittent; support estimates a few percent of quote requests during the
  afternoon peak (13:00-17:00 UTC).
- First reports arrived on 2026-09-16, the day after the 1.8.0 release.
- Pressing "Get rates" again usually works after a short wait.
- Not reproduced in development.

## Logs seen so far

- App warnings for the period read
  `quote failed status=504 error=rates_unavailable`. They carry no request IDs,
  so they cannot be matched to individual checkout requests.
- The CarrierLink status page reported no incident for the period.

## Access

The production hosts, the proxy access logs, and the load balancer metrics are
in the restricted production account. The app team cannot reach them from
development machines; requests for data go through the ops on-call.
