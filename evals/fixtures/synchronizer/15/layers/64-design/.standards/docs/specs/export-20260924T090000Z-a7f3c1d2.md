# Technical Design

## Context

The scope corrects the examples in the CLI usage guide `docs/cli.md` (AC-001)
and keeps the command's established behavior as the reference. According to
`.standards/CONTEXT.md`, that behavior is defined by `src/cli.js`
(`parseArgs`, `USAGE`, `run`) and `src/csv.js`, and is covered by
`test/cli.test.js` and `test/csv.test.js`.

## Decision

No technical change. The established command-line interface is the contract
the guide documents; source, tests, and configuration stay as they are.

## Acceptance Coverage

- `AC-001`: No architectural impact. Satisfaction depends on Documenter
  correcting `docs/cli.md` against the established behavior in `src/cli.js`
  and `src/csv.js`, and on final review confirming it.

## Interfaces and Contracts

- Established and unchanged: `records-export <input.json>` with
  `-c`/`--columns`, `-o`/`--output`, `--delimiter`, `-h`/`--help`, and
  `-v`/`--version`; CSV with a header row and CRLF after every row, quoted per
  RFC 4180 relative to the chosen delimiter; exit status 0, 1, or 2 as
  described in `.standards/CONTEXT.md`.
