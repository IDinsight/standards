# Export Timeout

## Goal

Exports sometimes write to destinations that stop accepting data, such as a
mounted share that stalls. The export then never finishes and the caller waits
forever. Exports should give up after a bounded time and report that they
timed out, without changing what successful exports produce or how imports
behave.

## Constraints

- The CSV produced by a successful export stays exactly as it is.
- Callers that pass no timeout get a default.

## Non-goals

- Timeouts for imports.
- Retrying a timed-out export or resuming a partial one.
- Removing partial export files left at a destination.

## Work

### 1. Bounded exports

**Intent:** A stalled destination must not hang an export indefinitely.

**Done when:**

- `AC-001`: An export that has not finished within its timeout fails with a timeout error instead of waiting indefinitely; callers can set the timeout, and it defaults to 30 seconds.
- `AC-002`: An export that finishes within its timeout writes the same CSV as before this change.

**Depends on:** None

### 2. Imports unchanged

**Intent:** Imports share stream code with exports and must not change.

**Done when:**

- `AC-003`: Importing records keeps its current behavior; imports are not subject to a timeout.

**Depends on:** None

## Assumptions

- 30 seconds is long enough for the largest exports in use today.
