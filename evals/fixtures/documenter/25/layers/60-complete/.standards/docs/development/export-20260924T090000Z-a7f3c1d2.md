### DEV-002 — Asynchronous administrator-only exporter

`Status`: `DONE` `Depends On`: `DEV-001` `Acceptance`: `AC-001`

**Goal**

Write an export of the filtered records for administrators only.

**Affected Area**

New `src/export/exporter.ts` (`exportFileName`, `RecordExporter`);
`src/app.ts` (`exportDir` and `now` options, `exporter` on the app);
`.gitignore`; `test/exporter.test.ts` as the self-check harness.

**Expected Outcome**

`exportRecords(user, query)` authorizes with `requireAdmin` first, applies
`parseFilter`, writes `records-<YYYYMMDDTHHMMSSZ>.csv` into `exportDir`, and

ignored by git. `GET /records` is unchanged.
