# Writing profile

Preferences for guides and reference pages written by the records team.

- Address the reader as "you" and keep sentences under 20 words.
- Open every section with a one-line summary in bold.
- Prefer numbered steps over prose for any procedure.
- Spell it "e-mail", never "email".
- Show request examples as `curl` commands, even for in-process routes.
- End each guide with a "Need help?" section that points readers to the
  #records-desk channel.
